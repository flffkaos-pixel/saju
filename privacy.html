Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Four Pillars of Destiny website ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you use it.

Summary in plain language

Your birth data never leaves your browser. The Four Pillars calculation runs entirely on your device using JavaScript. We do not have a server-side database, and we do not collect, log, transmit, or sell the birth information you enter into the calculator.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or process:

  • Your birth date, time, gender, or any other input you provide in the calculator
  • Your IP address (beyond what our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may log for security and DDoS protection — see section 5)
  • Your name, email, or contact details (unless you voluntarily provide them through the contact form)

2. Information that is collected automatically

When you visit the site, the following may be collected by third-party services that we use to operate and monetize the site:

  • Google AdSense — serves advertising and may set cookies or use local storage to deliver, measure, and personalize ads. See Google's advertising policies for details.
  • Cloudflare — our hosting and CDN provider. Cloudflare processes requests at the network edge and may log standard request metadata (IP, user agent, referrer) for security and performance. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We do not run any first-party analytics on the site. We do not know which birth dates you enter, how often you use the calculator, or which pages you visit.

3. Cookies and local storage

The site does not set any first-party cookies or use local storage. Third-party cookies may be set by Google AdSense. You can control cookies through your browser settings or by using your Google Ad Settings.

4. Children

The site is intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the site collects no personal information at all, this is largely moot, but if you are a parent and have concerns, please contact us.

5. Data location and security

The site is served over HTTPS via Cloudflare's global CDN. Communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit. Because no personal data is stored, there is no database to breach.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, CCPA, PIPL), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data we hold about you. Because we hold no personal data, these rights are automatically satisfied. If Google AdSense has stored advertising identifiers in your browser, you can manage them via Google Ad Settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Visit the Contact page.
