About contacting us

We're a small, independent project. We welcome feedback, corrections to the interpretive content, bug reports, and partnership or press inquiries. Please read below before sending a message.

What we can help with

  • Corrections to the algorithm or interpretive text
  • Bug reports on the calculator (incorrect calculation, UI issue, etc.)
  • Suggestions for additional content or features
  • Press, academic, or partnership inquiries
  • Translation questions for non-English Saju / BaZi material

What we cannot help with

  • Personal Saju readings or consultations (the site is for self-exploration, not paid services)
  • Medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice
  • Relationship, career, or business predictions
  • Adversarial interpretation of the chart for conflict resolution
